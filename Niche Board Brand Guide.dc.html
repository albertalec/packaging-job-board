01 · Positioning
A network of precision job boards — one board per narrow profession, refreshed daily from employer career sites.
What we do
Specialists browse a board that only holds their discipline, then apply on the employer's own career site. Employers pin listings they already have. No middle layer, no reposting, no noise.
Why it earns trust
Because we subtract. Every board is small on purpose, and the value is in what we chose to leave out. The brand should always feel edited rather than aggregated.
Trusted advocate

02 · The symbol
Built on a 4-unit grid · stroke = 1 unit
Two crop marks. The job is what sits between them.
A navy corner opens the frame, a teal counter-corner closes it, and the space they leave empty is the point: this is a brand about editing down, not piling up. The two corners never touch — the gap is structural and must not be closed.
Navy corner — structure, the board itself.
Teal corner — the specialist, closing the match.
Rotation is fixed. Never mirror the pair, never rotate one corner independently, and never add a third.
Circle accent
the roundel
The roundel is the accent form: avatars, app icons, section markers, list bullets and stamps. Symbol sits at 56% of the disc, optically centred. Use one roundel per view — it is punctuation, not decoration.

Two added accents, one job each
Teal and amber can't carry a network of dozens of boards on their own — everything starts to look like the same board. Violet and clay sit at the same lightness and chroma as teal (oklch L .55 / C .11), so they mix without shouting. Use them to code board families and to warm up editorial pages. Never in the logo, never for links, never as a button fill.

10 · Voice
Friendly, not eager. Confident, not loud. One dry joke per page is plenty.
Write this
“Eleven new packaging roles this week.”
“Apply on the employer's site — we don't sit in the middle.”
“Small board. That's the point.”
Not this
“Thousands of opportunities await!”
“Revolutionising talent acquisition.”
“Don't miss out — apply today!!”
Say the number
Eleven roles beats “lots of roles”. Specificity is the whole product.
Name the niche
Talk about packaging, prosthetics, perfusion — never “careers”.
Credit the source
Always clear that the job and the application live with the employer.
Stop early
No exclamation marks, no closing pitch. End on the useful sentence.
The right jobs, not all the jobs.
